The OpenEverest Milvus provider now offers Milvus 2.6.15 and GPU bundles with
per-component images. You can select the bundle that matches your workload —
CPU or GPU — and control images at the component level.

Previously, image choices were bundled at the release level, with no dedicated
GPU option.

To learn more, visit the
[release notes](https://github.com/openeverest/provider-milvus/releases/tag/v0.1.1).
