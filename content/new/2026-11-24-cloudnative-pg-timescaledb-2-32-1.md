The CloudNativePG provider now ships TimescaleDB Community 2.32.1 builds for PostgreSQL 18.6 and 18.7. Time-series and analytics workloads on OpenEverest-managed PostgreSQL 18 clusters can enable TimescaleDB on current patch releases without pinning an older PostgreSQL version.

Alongside the standard builds, the provider publishes dedicated TimescaleDB images built against PostgreSQL 18.6 and 18.7, so extensions stay compatible as the engine is patched.

To learn more, see the [v0.4.2 release notes](https://github.com/openeverest/provider-cloudnative-pg/releases/tag/v0.4.2).
