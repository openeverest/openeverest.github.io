The CloudNativePG provider now runs CloudNativePG 1.30, so new and existing
PostgreSQL clusters managed through OpenEverest benefit from the latest
upstream operator release.

The upgrade also adopts OpenEverest's server-side apply workflow, keeping the
provider aligned with current OpenEverest internals.

To learn more, visit the
[release notes](https://github.com/AdityaPimpalkar/provider-cloudnative-pg/releases/tag/v0.3.1).
