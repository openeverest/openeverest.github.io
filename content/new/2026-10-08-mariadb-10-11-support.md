The OpenEverest MariaDB provider now supports MariaDB 10.11, giving you another
long-term-supported MariaDB series to choose from when provisioning clusters.

MariaDB 10.11 is available as a version option for new deployments starting
with provider release v0.1.8.

To learn more, visit the
[release notes](https://github.com/openeverest/provider-mariadb/releases/tag/v0.1.8).
