---

OpenEverest now supports TiDB 8.5.8 and 7.5.8 as engine versions for new TiDB clusters. TiDB 8.5.2 and 7.5.5 are deprecated with this provider release, so plan upgrades for existing clusters on those versions.

To learn more, see the [provider-tidb v0.2.5 release notes](https://github.com/openeverest/provider-tidb/releases/tag/v0.2.5).
