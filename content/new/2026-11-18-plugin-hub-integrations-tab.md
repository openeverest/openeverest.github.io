The OpenEverest Plugin Hub now includes a dedicated integrations tab for
monitoring extensions, collecting them in one browsable place instead of
mixing them into the general catalog. Finding the right monitoring
integration — and checking what is already installed — is now a one-click
step rather than a scroll through every extension.

Plugin Hub v0.1.18 ships as a multi-arch image (linux/amd64 and
linux/arm64). To learn more, visit the
[release page](https://github.com/openeverest/plugin-hub/releases/tag/v0.1.18).
