The KServe provider now delivers a fully working AI Gateway for managing model inference traffic and routing.

The AI Gateway provides a centralized way to handle traffic to your deployed models, enabling better control over how requests are routed to inference services running in your cluster.

AI Gateway support is available starting with provider-kserve v0.2.0. See the release notes for setup and configuration details.
