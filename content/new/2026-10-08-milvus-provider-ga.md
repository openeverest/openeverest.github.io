The Milvus provider for OpenEverest is now available. You can deploy and manage
Milvus vector database clusters from OpenEverest, with authentication via a
generated root credential, configurable network exposure, and full control over
Milvus components.

The provider also honours component pod scheduling policies and passed live
end-to-end testing before release, so pod placement follows the same rules as
the rest of your OpenEverest deployment.

To learn more, visit the
[release notes](https://github.com/openeverest/provider-milvus/releases/tag/v0.1.0).
