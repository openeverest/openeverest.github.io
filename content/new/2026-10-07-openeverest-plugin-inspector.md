OpenEverest introduces Plugin Inspector, a diagnostic plugin that displays core health information for the components related to your database cluster. You can now inspect pod selectors and see component status without switching to kubectl or another external tool.

Previously, troubleshooting cluster issues required dropping into the terminal, which slowed diagnosis for teams that manage many databases through the OpenEverest console.

Plugin Inspector is available now as a Helm chart from the Extension Hub. To learn more, visit the [Plugin Hub](https://docs.openeverest.io/).
