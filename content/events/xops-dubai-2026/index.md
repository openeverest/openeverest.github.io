[X-Ops Dubai 2026](https://dubai.xopsconference.com/agenda) is the first X-Ops
edition in the Middle East. Sergey Pronin talks at the Executive Summit about
automating database operations on Kubernetes — provisioning, scaling, failover,
and maintenance with operators — where automation works today, where it still
falls short, and what role AI can realistically play.

Valeria Bogatyreva and Peter Zaitsev from Percona are also speaking at the
Summit.
